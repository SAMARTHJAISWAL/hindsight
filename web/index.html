Your year in the terminal.

Your top commands, your favourite typo, your busiest hour, and your terminal personality, as a card you can share. Your history never leaves your computer.

npx github:SAMARTHJAISWAL/hindsight --card

Needs Node 18 or later. Works with zsh, bash, fish and PowerShell.

No Node? Paste your history instead.

It's processed right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

  1. Copy your history. On a Mac: pbcopy < ~/.zsh_history (zsh) or pbcopy < ~/.bash_history (bash). On Linux, open the same file in an editor. On Windows PowerShell: Get-Content (Get-PSReadLineOption).HistorySavePath | Set-Clipboard
  2. Paste it below, or drop the file here.
